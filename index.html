Masaya's Journey 🐯✨

The Voice that Crosses Borders

Masaya Horikawa (堀川雅也) is a powerhouse vocalist from Japan whose emotional range and brilliant stage presence have captured hearts across Asia. Known for his flawless pitch, soaring high notes, and passionate deliveries, he brings a deep sense of vulnerability to every song he sings.

His participation in high-profile singing programs like Japan's THE Karaoke ☆ Battle and the cross-border television sensation Han-Il King of Song / Korea-Japan Top Ten Show established him as a premier vocalist. Masaya performs not just with technique, but with his whole heart, sharing his raw humanity through every lyric.

Singing is the bridge that connects different languages, cultures, and souls. Let's walk this musical path together!
